What sincefirst does

A view count you can actually read.

sincefirst is a command-line program I run on my own computer. I give it a search or a handful of listing IDs, and it prints how many people have looked at each one per day since the seller first listed it — rather than the lifetime total Etsy shows, which has no time attached to it.

The thing it exists to get right

Etsy publishes a views figure on every search result. It is the one number on a listing that looks like evidence of interest, and on its own it cannot be read. Here are two listings that came back side by side in one measured search:

viewslisted fora day
a pottery template bundle33516 days20.9
an enamel pin3412,554 days0.13

Six views apart. A hundred and sixty-four times apart in what they mean. The same search again on rings turned up 24,287 views collected over twelve years sitting next to 23,815 collected over nine months.

Etsy prints the left-hand column and never the right-hand one. So this program never shows a view count without the age beside it, and sorts on the rate.

Three more things it is careful about

The age is when the seller first listed the item, not Etsy’s renewal date. Every one of the most-viewed listings in every search measured had been renewed that day. Dividing by the renewal date would give a rate of infinity for everything, every morning.

Zero views is two different facts. About a third of every search comes back with no views at all — 34, 38 and 39 out of 100 in the searches measured. Zero on something listed this morning says nothing; nobody has had the chance. Zero on something listed four years ago is the loudest number on the page. Anything under a fortnight old is shown without a rate at all, and between a fifth and a half of a search is usually that new.

The bars are logarithmic, and it says so. The rates measured run from 0.00 to 857 a day — more than five orders of magnitude. Drawn to a linear scale, everything except the single busiest listing would be a bar of no width, which would read as “nobody looks at any of these”. The axis is printed above the bars so the scale is not a secret.

What it will not tell you

Whether to buy anything. A busy listing is not a better one and an unseen listing is not a worse one — plenty of the quietest things measured were one-off vintage pieces, which is why people come to Etsy in the first place. It prints the number and the age and stops. There is a test that fails the build if a word like “trending” or “hidden gem” ever reaches the screen.
